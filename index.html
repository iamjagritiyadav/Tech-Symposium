Welcome to TECH@IIMB

Technology is moving fast. Understanding must move with it.

TECH@IIMB is the annual technology symposium convened by the Centre for Software and IT Management (CSITM) at IIM Bangalore. Across three days, scholars, practitioners, policymakers, founders, investors and students come together to examine how digital technologies are transforming organisations, markets, the state and society — and what these shifts mean for India.

The 2027 edition takes up The Next Technology Revolution: India at the Frontier, asking one central question: how can we better understand and responsibly shape the technologies changing India and the world?

Pre-events
Three days · three questions

From what is coming to what India should build.

Each day of TECH@IIMB 2027 asks its own question and builds on the one before.

Day 01

The Technology Frontier

What is coming? Expert sessions on emerging technologies, the launch of the CSITM Tech Report, a technology expo, Frontier Labs, product showcases and the NSRCEL product launch.

Day 1 programme Day 02

Technology Meets Sectors

What will it change? Panels, expert talks and roundtables across AI Tech, FinTech, HealthTech, EdTech, AgriTech, and ClimateTech & Energy.

Day 2 programme Day 03

India @ the Frontier

What should India build? A national keynote, the Lawmakers Conclave, the Great Technology Debate and the Bengaluru Declaration on Technology 2030.
