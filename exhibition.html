Hardware Foundry · Day 2

Prototypes you can touch.

A hands-on innovation space where students, startups and researchers showcase prototypes including sensors, drones, IoT boards and printed circuit boards. Visitors explore inventions through demonstrations and hands-on interactions.

Details
Frontier Futures Exchange · Day 1

Forecast what comes next in AI.

A prediction market where participants use free virtual tokens to forecast what comes next in AI — from innovations to model performance. Three rotating challenges — Machine vs Market, Scenario Flash and Forecast Duel — with a new round every 20 minutes and a Frontier Futures Index naming the most accurate forecasters.
