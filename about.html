Overview

The flagship technology symposium of IIM Bangalore.

TECH@IIMB is the annual technology symposium convened by the Centre for Software and IT Management (CSITM) at IIM Bangalore. Designed as a three-day gathering, it brings together scholars, practitioners, policymakers and founders to examine how digital technologies are transforming organisations, markets, the state and society, and what these shifts mean for India’s technology leadership.

As CSITM’s flagship annual convening, TECH@IIMB creates space for rigorous inquiry, grounded debate and meaningful exchange across sectors. This year, TECH@IIMB 2027 focuses on The Next Technology Revolution: India at the Frontier.

The central question

How can we better understand and responsibly shape the technologies changing India and the world?

Purpose

Why TECH@IIMB?

Understand it. Question it. Shape it.

The context

Why now?

The pace of technological change is challenging the institutions and frameworks needed to understand its wider consequences.

Technology

Systems at scale

Generative and agentic AI, tokenised assets and population-scale Digital Public Infrastructure are changing how organisations operate, how markets evolve and how services are delivered.

Consequences

Questions to examine

The organisational, ethical and developmental implications deserve careful examination alongside adoption.

Dialogue

A neutral forum

Cross-sector discussion can connect evidence, practical experience and public-interest considerations — thoughtful, evidence-led dialogue across research, industry and public policy.

The opportunity

Create a space where technological ambition is considered alongside evidence, context, and its effects on people and institutions.

The symposium framework

Three pillars.

Our approach

What makes it different?

Many technology events celebrate what is new. TECH@IIMB asks what it means. Its format, setting and focus are designed for depth, candour and relevance to India.

One forum. Many perspectives. A shared question.

Why CSITM?

The right place to convene this conversation.

CSITM is IIM Bangalore’s dedicated centre for research, teaching and engagement on software, technology and digital transformation. Its academic focus, cross-sector relationships and institutional setting make it well placed to convene TECH@IIMB.

Who takes part

Perspectives that rarely share a room.

TECH@IIMB 2027 reaches the people building, funding, studying and governing technology in India.
